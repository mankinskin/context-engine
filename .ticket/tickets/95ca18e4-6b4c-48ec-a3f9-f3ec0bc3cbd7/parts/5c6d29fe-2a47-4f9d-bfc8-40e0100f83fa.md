## Decision

Remove all references to `CHEAT_SHEET.md`; no cheat-sheet file will be created.

## Rationale

The cheat sheet is too hard to keep up to date. Its content goes stale faster than it is corrected, creating a maintenance burden without a dependable source of guidance.

## Scope Boundary

A replacement mechanism will be found separately. Designing or selecting that replacement is explicitly deferred and out of scope for this ticket.
