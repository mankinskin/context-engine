## Objective

Move `crane-cli` from `context-engine/tools/cli/crane-cli` to `workflow-tools/tools/cli/crane-cli`, preserve its history-transplant behavior, and register `crane` as an available workflow-tools source artifact.

## Done Condition

- The workflow-tools checkout owns the crate and its tests.
- Context-engine no longer lists the old crate as a workspace member.
- `artifacts.toml` exposes the relocated binary with a workflow-tools-relative source path.

## Validation

Run `cargo test` for crane-cli and its existing transplant dry-run coverage from the relocated crate.
