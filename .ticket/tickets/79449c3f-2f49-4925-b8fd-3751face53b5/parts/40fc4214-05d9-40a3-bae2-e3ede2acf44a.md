## Approved version-one contract

- The Explainer Agent may fully read repository content before creating its explanation.
- The Explainer Agent explains evidence and a bounded plan; the human executes the task.
- The first version performs no task mutation and does not delegate execution.
- A later execution-capable version requires renewed approval for a new target or changed outcome.
- Feedback is team-visible, retained under the ordinary repository lifecycle, and informs only later human-reviewed template changes.
- Pilot acceptance: five representative runs, mean explanation and result ratings at least 4/5, with zero approval-boundary violations.

Create a linked specification, define the feedback target and aggregation query, and author validation for the read-before-explain and feedback-persistence flows.
