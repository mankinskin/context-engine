## Acceptance Criteria

1. Migration preflight reports source, destination, blockers, touched paths, and rollback eligibility without mutation.
2. Apply successfully migrates populated ticket, spec, test, session, feedback, log, doc, rule, and audit stores in fixtures; post-apply reads resolve every migrated entity from the canonical path.
3. Both-present and destination-conflict cases fail without merging or deleting either store.
4. An induced failure leaves the legacy source readable and records a recoverable journal; resume and rollback are tested.
5. Context-engine production stores, submodule stores, and affected fixture stores are relocated; tracked configuration/docs name canonical paths and `git status` confirms no unintended `.worktrees` changes.
6. Migration evidence includes artifact read-back, not only command exit status.
