## Design

Expose a domain-aware migration command with preflight, apply, resume, and rollback semantics. Preflight validates source/destination state, git-worktree identity, lock/lease state, and references before writing. Apply moves only one domain store at a time, rescans the destination, reads back representative entities, and records a journal. Update tracked consumer stores and fixtures after command behavior is proven; do not touch active `.worktrees` copies as part of the source migration.
