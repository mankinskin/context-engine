## Strict Resolver Rollout — Ticket, Spec, Session, Test, Feedback Transports

Adopted `memory_kernel::workspace::resolve_consumer_store_root_from` (and a new `resolve_consumer_store_root` cwd-bound wrapper) in place of the lenient `resolve_requested_store_root[_from]` across:

- `ticket/src/cli/dispatch.rs` (`resolve_index_root`/`resolve_index_root_from`), with a new `CliRunError::ConsumerWorkspace` variant.
- `spec/src/cli/dispatch.rs` (`resolve_index_root`/`resolve_index_root_from`), with a new `CliRunError::ConsumerWorkspace` variant.
- `session/src/bin/session-mcp.rs` startup `resolve_store_root`.
- `test/crates/test-mcp/src/main.rs` startup `resolve_store_root`.
- `test/crates/test-cli/src/lib.rs` `run()` (store/log/spec root resolution), with a new `CliRunError::ConsumerWorkspace` variant.

Ambient (no explicit `--index-root`/`--workspace`/env override) resolution now rejects a superproject cwd with more than one descendant store instead of silently picking one; explicit-selector precedence is unchanged.
