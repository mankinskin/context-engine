## Requirements Amendment (review, 2026-08-26)

Adds to the frozen requirements above; does not replace them.

- Audit bypasses the shared resolver entirely today: `.audit` is hardcoded as three independent local constants (`INDEX_DIR` in [workflow-tools/audit/crates/audit-api/src/index.rs](../../../../workflow-tools/audit/crates/audit-api/src/index.rs), `AUDIT_INDEX_DIR` in [move_domain.rs](../../../../workflow-tools/audit/crates/audit-api/src/move_domain.rs), `STORE_DIR` in [audit-cli/src/cli.rs](../../../../workflow-tools/audit/crates/audit-cli/src/cli.rs)). Adoption must collapse all three into calls through the shared `memory_kernel::workspace` resolver, not just add a canonical branch beside one of them.
- [workflow-tools/test/crates/test-cli/src/lib.rs](../../../../workflow-tools/test/crates/test-cli/src/lib.rs) derives the log store root as `store_root.parent().join(LOG_STORE_DIR)` — a sibling-of-`.test` join that assumes a flat workspace layout. Adoption must replace this call site with a direct resolver call for the log domain; once `.test` nests under `.workflow-tools/test/`, this join would silently resolve to `.workflow-tools/.log` instead of `.workflow-tools/log`.
