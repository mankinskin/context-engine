## Scope Decision

The requested continuation is scope **(a)** — reconcile the roadmap and identify the migration-tooling execution slice, gated on explicit approval before execution — and no alternative scope is better supported by the evidence gathered. The instructed guardrail (no execution without explicit approval) is honored: no code, ticket, spec, `README.md`, or `ROADMAP.md` was edited by this review.

However, "reconcile" cannot be a light pass over `ROADMAP.md`'s existing prose. Findings 1–2 show the roadmap's Waypoint 6 framing and its entity links are stale relative to live state, not merely imprecise. The migration-tooling execution slice this task must identify is concretely ticket `30bbbace` ("[spec-system][W6.1] Implement v2 model, storage, and explicit migration", `state: open`, workspace `.workflow-tools`) — not a slice to be newly scoped or ticketed, contrary to `ROADMAP.md`'s current wording. Its actual blocking dependency needs a decision before it is dispatchable (finding 3).
